In today's fast-paced world, many people have opinions about remote work. Remote work has been adopted by lots of companies. Meetings are held on video calls and emails are sent all day. Productivity has been studied by researchers and it was found to be higher.

Costs are also reduced. Offices do not need to be rented and commuting is not required. Employees are said to be happier.

In conclusion, there are many things to think about with remote work.
