# House style

One authoritative copy of the writing rules. Findings and checks use these IDs.

| ID | Rule | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ESS-01 | State the thesis in the first paragraph, in one sentence. | The reader knows what is being argued before the evidence arrives. |
| ESS-02 | One idea per paragraph, opened by a topic sentence. | Each paragraph can be checked against the thesis. |
| ESS-03 | Support every factual claim, or mark it `[citation needed]`. Never invent sources, quotes or statistics. | Honest sourcing. An invented source is worse than none. |
| ESS-04 | Prefer active voice and plain words. Keep sentences under 30 words. | Easier to read and easier to challenge. |
| ESS-05 | No filler openers or closers such as "In today's fast-paced world" or "In conclusion". | They add length and no meaning. |
| ESS-06 | Use Australian English spelling (organisation, analyse, colour). | Consistent with the audience. |
| ESS-07 | Finish by restating the thesis in new words and saying what follows from it. | The ending should land, not repeat. |

## Severity

- **High:** ESS-01 and ESS-03.
- **Medium:** ESS-02, ESS-04 and ESS-07.
- **Low:** ESS-05 and ESS-06.
