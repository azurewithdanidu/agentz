# Outline template

```text
Working title: <title>
Audience: <who reads this>
Length: <words>
Thesis (one sentence): <the position being argued>

1. Introduction: hook, context, thesis
2. Section: <point>, evidence: <what supports it>
3. Section: <point>, evidence: <what supports it>
4. Section: <counter-argument and response>
5. Conclusion: restate thesis in new words, what follows

Open questions: <anything the user should decide>
```

End with: "Happy with this outline? Say go ahead and I'll draft it, or tell me what to change."
