# Feedback report template

```text
Essay: <file or title>
Summary: <one sentence on the overall state of the draft>

| Rule ID | Severity | Finding | Quote from the draft |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ESS-01 | High | <what is wrong> | "<exact quote>" |

Strengths: <one or two things that work>
Suggested next step: <the single most useful change>
```

Severity comes from [../references/house-style.md](../references/house-style.md). List findings from High to Low. Do not rewrite the draft.
